Most of what an agency charges for is the agency.

An account manager briefs a project manager, who briefs a developer. You pay for all three, and two of them exist to coordinate the third. What you get here is one person for the part that has to be right and stay right: the system, the data, and the day-to-day of keeping it running. Nothing falls between three suppliers, because there aren't three suppliers.

A product built from nothing

You've got a business and an idea, and nothing built yet. I turn that into something your customers can sign up to and use: the site or app they see, plus the accounts, the payments and the admin screens you run it from.

02

Pages that load in under a second

If your site has got slower as it's grown, that's usually weeks of work to fix rather than months. Faster pages mean fewer people give up before they buy.

Software nobody is looking after

The developer who built it has gone, or the agency moved on. I take it over, make it safe to change again, and write down how it works, so you're never stuck like this twice.

04

Software that keeps working

Automatic checks, a release that takes about a minute, and documentation in plain English. A change made on a Friday afternoon shouldn't take the business down on Saturday.
